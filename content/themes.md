# Themes

Every item is tagged with themes in four categories. Theme names must match exactly (case and punctuation) across items.


## Topics

- **Behavioral & mental health** (9 items): Behavioral health, ABA, digital mental health, crisis response and suicide prevention
- **Health equity & SDOH** (10 items): Health equity, social determinants of health and public health
- **Assistive tech & AAC** (5 items): Assistive technology and augmentative & alternative communication (AAC) devices
- **Disability, neurodiversity & inclusion** (8 items): Disability, neurodiverse care and inclusive design
- **Youth & development** (8 items): Youth wellbeing, child and adolescent development, child welfare
- **Online safety & misinformation** (3 items): Youth social-media harms, hate speech, disinformation
- **Responsible AI & policy** (13 items): Algorithmic accountability, ethics in technology, privacy, compliance and tech policy
- **Applied AI** (8 items): Building with and evaluating AI and machine learning
- **Healthcare technology** (2 items): Clinical software, EMR/SaaS products, clinical documentation and health data infrastructure
- **Global & cross-cultural work** (10 items): Global development, food security, immigration, culture and identity
- **Psychology & cognition** (3 items): Cognitive, social, personality and developmental psychology

- **Data science & visualization** (8 items): Data cleaning and integration, dashboards, Tableau, Excel
