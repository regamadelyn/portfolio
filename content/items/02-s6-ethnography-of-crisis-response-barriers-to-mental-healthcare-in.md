## Question
What human, cultural and logistical factors affect access to mental healthcare in the Southeastern U.S., and how can communities be supported to prevent suicide?

## Methods
Contextual inquiry: passive observation of a nine-member SAMHSA expert panel (federal and state officials, first responders, peer specialists) on the 988 Lifeline. Took fieldnotes, built a codebook (including an observer-positionality code for reflexivity) and coded inductively by theme, then compared findings with the parallel quantitative model (S1).

## Findings
Most frequent codes: continuum of care (17), new predictor variables (14), at-risk communities (13), systemic barriers (12). Surfaced predictors missing from the model (language isolation, LGBTQIA status, veteran status, farming-related economic instability, fear of police response) and turned them into design requirements for a public-health dashboard: co-responder mapping, continuum-of-care metrics and mobile service areas.
