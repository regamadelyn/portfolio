## Question
What happens when a predictive algorithm drives post-acute care and claims decisions, and how should its design and governance change?

## Methods
Analyzed nH Predict through techno-centric, human-centric and collective-intelligence perspectives at micro, meso and macro levels, drawing on investigative reporting, litigation and company claims.

## Findings
Recommended explainable AI, a protected clinician-override workflow, multidisciplinary review of AI recommendations, clinician training, and regulatory transparency and monitoring requirements.
