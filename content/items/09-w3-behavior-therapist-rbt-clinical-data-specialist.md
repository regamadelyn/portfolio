## Problem
Nonvocal children with autism needed functional, independent communication, and challenging behaviors needed data-driven intervention.

## Process
Taught communication with speech-generating devices, AAC systems, PECS and adaptive switches; collected and analyzed high-frequency longitudinal behavioral data under clinical standards; delivered in-home therapy in Spanish and English for underserved families.

## Result
Clients went from 0–5 vocal words to fluent sentence communication; 83% reduction in challenging behaviors across a multi-client caseload.
