## Question
How does algorithmic amplification expose adolescents to disinformation, cyberbullying and hate speech, and how can they learn to recognize online manipulation?

## Methods
Investigated developmental harms of youth social media use; designed and prototyped a gamified educational app in Python, with Figma wireframes and prototypes (missions such as Melt Hate Speech, Mix Up Misinformation and Send Bullies to Saturn; Learn the Lingo term cards; quizzes and points).

## Findings
A working prototype that trains adolescents to identify online manipulation and harmful propaganda.
