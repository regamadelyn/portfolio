## Problem
Clinical school consultation teams lacked a unified source of client data, and operational bottlenecks slowed case resolution and family access to services.

## Process
Designed and implemented a centralized client management database with automated task workflows; ran structured user interviews and UAT to turn stakeholder needs into engineering requirements; built research-informed outreach strategies. As Case Manager (May – Jun 2024), restructured cross-departmental case resolution pipelines.

## Result
11 new institutional partnerships, evaluated through quarterly reviews; faster case resolution and better family access to behavioral services.
