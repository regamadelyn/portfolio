## Question
How do behavior technicians and analysts experience and integrate data collection apps during ABA sessions, and what would make these tools better?

## Methods
Literature review on data collection integrity in ABA; scoped research plan and interview guide; six semi-structured interviews (5 RBTs, 1 BCBA; Teams and in person, 30–45 min, recorded with consent); transcribed, coded inductively and organized into a codebook of categories and subcategories, with reflexivity notes on insider bias.

## Findings
Most technicians use a hybrid of paper and app. Key barriers: clients reacting to devices, battery and offline failures, no undo, hard-to-find notes. Experienced technicians want visibility into progress trends and the treatment plan. Recommended free-text notes, offline mode, company devices, and BT–BCBA change logs and progress visualizations.
